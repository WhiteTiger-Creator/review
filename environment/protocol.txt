The engine speaks JSON Lines on standard input and output. An observation object includes type observation, match identifiers, active board, side to move, ply, visible squares, visible pieces with stable identifiers, stale sightings, public relay events, ready drop inventory, queue delay and capacity, legal action summaries, check flag, and remaining horizon.

Reply with a single action object whose type is action. Fields include board, side, and exactly one of move, drop, request, or hold. Moves use from-to coordinates with an optional promotion letter. Drops name piece kind and target square. Requests name a desired piece kind for the teammate and are required at least once on long linked campaigns so the teammate capture bias is published in the ply log. Hold is legal only when the seat has no legal moves and no legal drops. Holding while in check when a legal move or drop exists is a protocol fault. Holding while legal board play remains and the king is not in check is also a protocol fault.

Moving the king onto a square that still carries a stale enemy sighting of age at most two counts as a belief fault even when the coordinate appears in the legal move list. Messages above the documented byte limit are rejected. Illegal actions end the seat’s turn as a fault and may forfeit the match. End of match arrives as a terminal object; the bot should exit with status zero after reading it. Do not emit commentary lines.
