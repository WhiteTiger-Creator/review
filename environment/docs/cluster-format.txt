Cluster snapshots use one key equals value per line. Every required key appears exactly once; unknown, duplicate, missing, and malformed fields are rejected.

Required keys are id epoch observed_epoch health nearfull backfillfull dest_used dest_capacity move_bytes source_osd dest_osd source_host dest_host source_rack dest_rack replica_hosts replica_racks current_weight target_weight max_step external_flags owned_flags journal_stage recovery_queue and client_iops.

id, OSD, host, and rack identifiers contain only letters, digits, dots, dashes, or underscores. health is clean or degraded. journal_stage is none, flagged, reweighted, or migrated.

epoch, observed_epoch, dest_used, recovery_queue, and client_iops are nonnegative decimal integers. dest_capacity and move_bytes are positive decimal integers, and dest_used does not exceed dest_capacity.

nearfull, backfillfull, current_weight, target_weight, and max_step are decimal values from zero through one. nearfull is less than backfillfull and max_step is positive.

replica_hosts and replica_racks are nonempty comma separated lists of equal length with no duplicates. external_flags and owned_flags are comma separated safe flag names and may be empty.

Epoch is the current monitor epoch and observed_epoch is the policy view. Replica hosts and racks describe surviving placement group members. External flags belong to another operator, while owned flags belong to this maintenance window.
