The policy prints one command per line.

The first command is epoch followed by the observed integer epoch.
Flag nobackfill owned records a temporary flag created by this window.
Tune max_backfills or recovery_priority is followed by an integer.
Reweight is followed by the target OSD and a decimal weight.
Move is followed by a positive byte count.
Checkpoint is followed by reweighted or migrated.
Clear nobackfill removes only a flag owned by this window.
Hold is followed by exactly one reason from stale-epoch, degraded-health, foreign-flag, fullness-gate, failure-domain, flag-policy, or minimum-weight.
Publish is followed by paused, staged, or complete and must be last.
Decision priority is stale-epoch, flag-policy, a migrated resume, degraded-health, foreign-flag, minimum-weight, fullness-gate, failure-domain, then accepted tuning and movement.
