artists.csv defines unique artist_id keys and artist_type values. tags.csv defines unique non-geographic tag_id keys. Country names, nationality words, place names, and location-derived genre names were removed uniformly before the public and hidden graphs were split. artist_tags.csv contains unique artist_id,tag_id bipartite edges whose endpoints must exist. train_labels.csv maps distinct artists to DE, GB, or US, with every class present. queries.csv names distinct artists disjoint from training labels. Every query has at least one tag, but some query tags may have no labeled neighbor.

The learned model uses tag neighborhoods and may use artist_type. Statistics, parameters, vocabulary weights, priors, and calibration choices are fitted from labeled artists only. Query graph membership supports inference but query countries are withheld. Any deterministic classifier is admissible if it learns from the labeled bipartite network, predicts every withheld artist, remains defined for cold tags, and meets the output contract. CSV row order does not alter predictions.

The quality contract uses balanced accuracy and mean multiclass log loss together. Ordinary, row-permuted, opaque-identifier, and cold-tag bundles require values of at least 0.58 and at most 1.40 respectively. The corresponding limits are 0.52 and 1.80 for a rotated training split, and 0.45 and 1.90 for a complete artist-kind holdout.

Output classes are ordered DE,GB,US. predictions.csv contains artist_id,prob_DE,prob_GB,prob_US,predicted_country for each query and no other artist. Probabilities are finite, nonnegative, and sum to one. The predicted country is the largest probability, with ties following class order.
