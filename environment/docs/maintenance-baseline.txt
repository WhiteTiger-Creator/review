The editable settings are ceph.conf, fullness.conf, recovery.conf, reweight.conf, and flags.conf beneath /app/work/ceph/etc.

ceph.conf keeps osd_max_backfills at 1, osd_recovery_op_priority at 2, and osd_recovery_sleep at 0.05.
recovery.conf uses high_client_iops 8000 and high_queue 24. Equality is busy.
Busy recovery uses 1 backfill and priority 2, while quiet recovery uses 3 backfills and priority 5.
reweight.conf uses maximum_step 0.15 and minimum_weight 0.05.
The policy uses the smaller of the cluster max_step and configured maximum_step.
A target below minimum_weight pauses with minimum-weight.
fullness.conf uses backfill_headroom_ratio 0.01 and nearfull_margin_ratio 0.01.
flags.conf uses maintenance_flag nobackfill and clear_only_owned true.
Any clear_only_owned value other than true pauses with flag-policy before mutation.
