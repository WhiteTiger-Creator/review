The journal describes work accepted before a process restart.

None begins a new window.
Flagged means the window already owns nobackfill.
Reweighted means the accepted weight is already active and movement is next.
Migrated means bytes already moved and bypasses new health, fullness, topology, reweight, and move decisions.
A migrated restart emits epoch, optionally clears nobackfill only when owned, then publishes complete.
Never repeat an accepted reweight or movement after restart.
