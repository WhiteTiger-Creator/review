THE LINK RULES
INDEX AND NAVIGATION MAP

The link rules are numbered N001 through N160, contiguous across five files. This index says
where every family stands so that no clause has to be hunted for. THE INDEX CARRIES NO CLAUSE
OF ITS OWN; every binding clause lives in one of the files named below.

Several of these clauses settle matters DIFFERENTLY from the way bonds, VLAN interfaces and
duplicate addresses are ordinarily run on other hosts. Each one says so where it stands, and
the eleven that most often surprise a reader who has run this kind of host elsewhere are listed
at the end of this index with the clauses that state them. Nothing carried in from another host
decides a state here.

--- Where these rules part company with ordinary practice ---

A bond under its member floor does NOT go down: it stays up in a
   reduced standing whose three consequences are given once           N092, N094
Reduced standing is STICKY and survives its members returning; only
   a raise ever clears it                                             N083, N093
A VLAN is ruled with NO reference to its parent at all                N115, N119
An address record is ruled with NO reference to whether the interface
   it names stands                                                    N133
A duplicate address is settled by reduced standing, then by DEPTH,
   then by the LAST record declared; the moment of claim is never
   weighed                                                            N135
The bond bears on the BOTTOM-most counting member                     N068
The carry NEVER fails back while its holder still counts              N069, N070
A member attached during the run joins the TOP of the ladder, which
   is the coldest place                                               N051
A member whose link drops STAYS enrolled at its unchanged place       N057, N058
A raise REBUILDS the ladder from the member table and discards what
   the run gathered                                                   N081
A member two bonds declare goes to the LOWER bond in the table, which
   takes it away from a bond already holding it                       N044
