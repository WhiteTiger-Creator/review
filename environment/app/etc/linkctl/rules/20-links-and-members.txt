THE LINK RULES
LINKS, LADDERS, MEMBERS AND THE CARRY (N035 through N078)

These clauses settle which links stand, which members a bond holds and in what order, which of
them count, and which single member bears for the bond. They are weighed before the bond
standing clauses at N079 and are what those clauses read.

--- Link standing across the run (N035 through N040) ---

N035  At every moment of the run a physical interface's link either STANDS or does not. At the
open it stands where the interface table wrote it up and does not where the table wrote it
down, by N010.

N036  A LINK-DOWN STEP names one physical interface. Its link stops standing from that instant.
A LINK-DOWN STEP ON A LINK THAT ALREADY DOES NOT STAND CHANGES NOTHING ANYWHERE.

N037  A LINK-UP STEP names one physical interface. Its link stands from that instant. A LINK-UP
STEP ON A LINK THAT ALREADY STANDS CHANGES NOTHING ANYWHERE.

N038  A LINK-UP STEP ON A LINK THAT HAS NEVER STOOD makes it stand from that instant, and from
that instant onward it has stood at some moment. Such an interface is therefore NOT UNWIRED at
the close, whatever its link does afterwards.

N039  An interface HAS STOOD AT SOME MOMENT where the interface table wrote it up at the open,
or where a link-up step reached it at any point of the run. This reading is never undone by a
later link-down step.

N040  Nothing else moves a link's standing. A bond step, a VLAN step and an address step leave
every link exactly as they found it, and a link that is not named by a link step keeps its
standing from the open to the close.

--- The ladder and what stands on it (N041 through N048) ---

N041  A bond's LADDER is an ordered list of the physical interfaces enrolled in it, read from
the TOP downward. The member at the top holds place one, the next holds place two, and so on
down. The member standing LOWEST on the ladder is the one holding the highest place number,
that is, the BOTTOM-MOST member.

N042  A PHYSICAL INTERFACE STANDS ON AT MOST ONE LADDER AT ANY MOMENT. This holds whether or
not the bond holding it stands: a bond that has been lowered still holds the members its ladder
carries, by N085, and a member it holds is a member no other bond can take except under N044.

N043  ENROLMENT AT A RAISE. When a bond is raised its ladder is built afresh from the member
table. Its declared members are taken IN DECLARED LADDER ORDER, top line first, and each one in
turn is weighed under the enrolment rule at N044. A member the rule refuses is simply left off
and the ladder is that much shorter; the members that follow it are still weighed.

N044  THE ENROLMENT RULE, and it is the same rule at a raise and at an attach step. THIS IS ONE
OF THE PLACES THESE RULES PART COMPANY WITH ORDINARY PRACTICE, where the bond that claimed a
device first is usually the one that keeps it. Here the two cases are weighed in this order:
      FIRST, where the member stands on no ladder at all, the enrolling bond takes it and
      nothing else is weighed.
      SECOND, where the member already stands on ANOTHER bond's ladder, the bond standing LOWER
      IN THE BOND TABLE takes it. So where the enrolling bond stands lower in the table than
      the bond holding the member, the member is REMOVED from the holder's ladder and enrolled
      in the enrolling bond. Where the enrolling bond stands HIGHER in the table than the
      holder, the enrolment takes nothing at all, the holder keeps the member unchanged, and
      the enrolling bond's ladder is shorter by one.
Lower in the bond table means further down the file, by N014.

N045  Where the member already stands on the ENROLLING bond's own ladder, the enrolment takes
and the member's place is settled by the clause that called for it: N043 at a raise, N051 at an
attach.

N046  A bond may enrol a member the member table does not declare for it, but only through an
attach step. A raise enrols the declared members and no others.

N047  Where the enrolment rule takes a member away from a bond that held it, that bond's carry
is given up at that instant if the member taken was the one bearing, by N071. The bond keeps
every other member on its ladder, at their unchanged places.

N048  The enrolment rule weighs the LADDERS and not the standing of any bond. A bond that does
not stand still holds its members against a higher bond, and a bond that does not stand is
still weighed as the holder in N044.

--- Attach and detach (N049 through N056) ---

N049  An ATTACH STEP names a bond and a physical interface.

N050  AN ATTACH STEP ON A BOND THAT DOES NOT STAND DOES NOTHING AT ALL. A bond that has not
been raised, or that has been lowered and not raised since, enrols nobody.

N051  Where the bond stands and the enrolment rule at N044 takes the member, THE MEMBER JOINS
THE TOP OF THE LADDER, at place one, pushing every other member down one place. THIS IS ONE OF
THE PLACES THESE RULES PART COMPANY WITH ORDINARY PRACTICE, where a device put back into an
assembly usually returns to its declared place. It joins the top and never its declared place
and never the bottom. Under N068 the top is the COLDEST place on the ladder: a member at the
top bears only where it is the last member counting.

N052  AN ATTACH STEP NAMING A MEMBER THAT BOND ALREADY HOLDS moves that member to the TOP of
the ladder. It is not doubled and it is not left where it was.

N053  AN ATTACH STEP NAMING A MEMBER ANOTHER BOND HOLDS is settled by the enrolment rule at
N044, exactly as at a raise. Where the attaching bond stands lower in the bond table it takes
the member and puts it at the top of its own ladder. Where it does not, THE ATTACH TAKES
NOTHING, both ladders are unchanged, and nothing else in the estate moves.

N054  A DETACH STEP names a bond and a member. Where that bond's ladder holds that member, the
member LEAVES THE LADDER AT THAT INSTANT, whether or not the bond stands, and the members below
it move up one place each. The bond's carry is given up if the member detached was the one
bearing, by N071.

N055  A DETACH STEP NAMING A MEMBER THAT BOND DOES NOT HOLD CHANGES NOTHING ANYWHERE, whether
the member stands on another bond's ladder or on none.

N056  A member that has left a bond by a detach step at any moment of the run HAS LEFT A BOND
IN THE RUN, and N149 reads that when it chooses between the two BARE tokens. It is enrolled
again only where a later attach step or a later raise enrols it.

--- A member whose link drops (N057 through N060) ---

N057  A MEMBER WHOSE LINK GOES DOWN STAYS ENROLLED, AT ITS UNCHANGED PLACE ON THE LADDER. THIS
IS ONE OF THE PLACES THESE RULES PART COMPANY WITH ORDINARY PRACTICE, where a device that loses
carrier is released from its assembly and taken back when carrier returns. Here only two things
ever take a member off a ladder: a detach step by N054, and a raise rebuilding the ladder by
N081. Nothing else does.

N058  A member whose link does not stand STOPS COUNTING for its bond while the link is down,
and COUNTS AGAIN the instant its link comes back, at its unchanged place.

N059  A member whose link comes back NEVER MOVES ON THE LADDER. It does not go to the top, it
does not go to the bottom, and it does not go to its declared place. It is where it already
was.

N060  The PLACE printed for a member in the report is its place on the ladder at the close,
counted from the top, one for the top. An interface no ladder holds at the close prints a
single hyphen in that column and a single hyphen in the bond column.

--- The three member columns (N061 through N064) ---

N061  THE ENROLLED COLUMN of a bond is the number of members standing on its ladder at the
close, whatever their links are doing and whether or not the bond stands.

N062  THE COUNTED COLUMN of a bond is the number of members on its ladder WHOSE LINK STANDS.
A member counts where it is enrolled AND its link stands, and in no other case.

N063  THE COUNTED COLUMN IS THE ONE WEIGHED AGAINST THE MEMBER FLOOR, at N088, and against the
declared count at N097. The enrolled column is never weighed against either; it is printed and
nothing more.

N064  THE DECLARED COLUMN of a bond is the number of members the member table declares for it,
by N022. It never changes during the run.

--- The carry (N065 through N074) ---

N065  THE CARRY. At most one member of a bond BEARS for it at any moment. The member bearing is
the one holding the bond's carry. Where a bond's carry is held by no member the carry STANDS
VACANT.

N066  A bond's carry can be held at all only where these three hold together, and they are
weighed in this order: FIRST the bond stands; SECOND at least one member is enrolled on its
ladder; THIRD the bond is not in reduced standing, by N094. Where any one of the three fails
the carry stands vacant and no member bears for that bond.

N067  Where the carry stands vacant, every enrolled member of that bond whose link stands is
SPARE and carries the token spare.vacant, by N149. None of them bears.

N068  WHO TAKES A VACANT CARRY. Of the members whose links stand and which therefore count, the
one standing LOWEST ON THE LADDER, that is, the BOTTOM-MOST counting member, takes the carry.
THIS IS ONE OF THE PLACES THESE RULES PART COMPANY WITH ORDINARY PRACTICE, where an assembly
bears on a nominated primary or on the first available member from the top. Here the direction
is downward and the bottom-most counting member is the one that bears.

N069  THE CARRY NEVER MOVES WHILE THE MEMBER HOLDING IT STILL COUNTS. THIS IS ONE OF THE PLACES
THESE RULES PART COMPANY WITH ORDINARY PRACTICE, where an assembly fails back to its primary as
soon as the primary returns. Here the carry is taken afresh under N068 ONLY at a moment when no
member holds it, and never at any other moment.

N070  It follows that A MEMBER WHOSE LINK COMES BACK BELOW THE BEARER TAKES NOTHING, even
though it now stands lower on the ladder than the member bearing. The bearer holds the carry
until it stops counting or the carry is given up under N071.

N071  A bond's carry is GIVEN UP at once, and stands vacant from that instant, at any of these
moments: the link of the member holding it goes down; that member is detached by N054; that
member is taken away by the enrolment rule at N044; the bond is lowered by N085; the bond is
raised by N082; or the bond enters reduced standing by N092. At the same instant the carry may
be taken afresh by N068 where the bond may still bear.

N072  The carry of every bond is weighed after the open and after every step of the run, in the
order of work at N075.

N073  A bond none of whose enrolled members has a standing link has NO COUNTING MEMBER, so its
carry stands vacant. This is true whatever its floor is and whatever its state.

N074  THE BEARER COLUMN of the bond block prints the name of the member bearing for that bond
at the close, or a SINGLE HYPHEN where the carry stands vacant.

--- The order of work, and what the run is not (N075 through N078) ---

N075  THE ORDER OF WORK AT EVERY STEP, weighed in this order and no other:
      FIRST the step itself is applied in full, by the clause for its kind.
      SECOND every bond is weighed against its member floor, IN BOND TABLE ORDER, by N092.
      THIRD the carry of every bond is settled, IN BOND TABLE ORDER, by N066 and N068.

N076  The same order of work is done ONCE AT THE OPEN, after every bond written raised at the
open has been raised in bond table order, and before step one is read. N034 says so again.

N077  No state of any interface, bond, VLAN or record is decided during the run. Every state in
the report is read off THE CLOSING ARRANGEMENT, after the last step has been weighed, except
where a clause says outright that it reads something that happened at some moment: whether a
link ever stood, whether a bond was ever raised, whether a VLAN was ever raised, whether a
record was ever claimed, whether a member ever left a bond, and whether a bond ever fell under
its floor without being raised since.

N078  THE STEPS OF THE RUN TAKE NO RULING OF THEIR OWN. No step is given a state, no step is
counted, no step is listed and no count of steps appears anywhere in the report. The run exists
only to move persistent hardware and standing configuration from one arrangement to another.
