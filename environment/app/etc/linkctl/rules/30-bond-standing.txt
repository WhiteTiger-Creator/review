THE LINK RULES
RAISING, LOWERING, THE MEMBER FLOOR AND THE BOND STATE LADDER (N079 through N112)

--- Raise and lower (N079 through N086) ---

N079  A bond STANDS where it has been raised and has not been lowered since. At the open it
stands where the bond table wrote it yes, by N017.

N080  A RAISE STEP naming a bond makes that bond stand from that instant, and from that instant
onward it HAS BEEN RAISED AT SOME MOMENT. That reading is never undone by a later lower step.

N081  A RAISE REBUILDS THE LADDER. At the instant of a raise the bond's ladder is emptied and
built afresh from the member table, in declared ladder order, under the enrolment rule at N044.
THIS IS ONE OF THE PLACES THESE RULES PART COMPANY WITH ORDINARY PRACTICE, where bringing an
assembly back up restores the membership it had. EVERYTHING THE RUN GATHERED ON THAT LADDER IS
DISCARDED AT THAT INSTANT: a member attached during the run and not declared by the member
table is dropped, a member moved to the top by an attach step goes back to its declared place,
and a member another bond took away is taken back where N044 allows it.

N082  A raise gives up the bond's carry, by N071. The carry is then taken afresh under N068 in
the same order of work.

N083  A RAISE CLEARS REDUCED STANDING. At the instant of a raise the bond is no longer in
reduced standing, and its standing is weighed afresh from that instant by N092. THIS IS THE
ONLY WAY REDUCED STANDING IS EVER CLEARED; N093 says so again.

N084  A raise step on a bond that already stands does every one of these things again: the
ladder is rebuilt, the carry is given up and retaken, and reduced standing is cleared.

N085  A LOWER STEP naming a bond makes that bond stop standing from that instant, and its carry
is given up by N071. ITS LADDER IS LEFT EXACTLY AS IT STANDS: no member is released, every
member keeps its place, and the ladder is rebuilt only at a later raise. A member of a lowered
bond therefore still prints that bond and that place in the interface block, and still stands
on a ladder for the purposes of N042 and N044.

N086  A BOND LOWERED AND RAISED AGAIN BEFORE THE CLOSE IS NOT DOWN. Every state in this
rulebook speaks only of the close, by N077. At that raise its ladder was rebuilt by N081 and
its reduced standing was cleared by N083.

--- The member floor and reduced standing (N087 through N096) ---

N087  A bond's member floor is read from the bond table by N015 and never changes.

N088  A bond is UNDER ITS FLOOR at a moment where all three of these hold: it stands; at least
one member is enrolled on its ladder; and its COUNTED COLUMN, by N062, is BELOW its member
floor. The counted column is the one weighed, never the enrolled column and never the declared
count.

N089  A BOND WHOSE MEMBER FLOOR IS ZERO IS NEVER UNDER ITS FLOOR, because a count of whole
members is never below zero. Such a bond is never in reduced standing and is never STINTED. It
is FULL or THIN while it holds members, and DOWN while it holds none.

N090  A bond that does not stand is not weighed against its floor at any moment. It cannot
enter reduced standing while it is down.

N091  A bond standing with NO MEMBER ENROLLED AT ALL is not weighed against its floor either.
The no-member test at N097 settles it, and that test is weighed FIRST.

N092  ENTRY INTO REDUCED STANDING. The instant a bond is under its floor by N088 it enters
REDUCED STANDING. THIS IS ONE OF THE PLACES THESE RULES PART COMPANY WITH ORDINARY PRACTICE,
where an assembly that falls below its minimum member count loses carrier and takes its
addresses down with it. HERE THE BOND DOES NOT GO DOWN. It stays up, it keeps every member on
its ladder, it keeps every address record declared on it, and it stands in a reduced standing
whose three consequences are given at N094 and nowhere else.

N093  REDUCED STANDING IS STICKY. THIS IS ONE OF THE PLACES THESE RULES PART COMPANY WITH
ORDINARY PRACTICE, where an assembly recovers the moment enough members come back. MEMBERS
RETURNING DO NOT CLEAR IT. A bond that has been under its floor and has since regained every
member it lost is STILL in reduced standing at the close and is STILL STINTED, and its token
says which of the two happened, by N103. It is cleared at ONE moment only: a raise, by N083. So
a lower step followed by a raise step clears it, a raise on a bond that already stands clears
it, and nothing else does.

N094  WHAT REDUCED STANDING DOES. THREE THINGS HOLD AND NO OTHERS:
      FIRST the bond's carry stands vacant, by N066, so no member bears for it and every
      standing member of it is SPARE with spare.vacant.
      SECOND of the records declared on that bond and claimed at the close, every one except
      its top-ranked claimed record is SET-ASIDE, by N129.
      THIRD the one surviving claimed record on it is weighed LAST in the address contest, by
      rung one of N135.
Nothing else changes. In particular the bond's members keep their links, their places and their
enrolment, the bond keeps its VLANs, and every VLAN over it is ruled without reference to it by
N115.

N095  A bond in reduced standing STILL STANDS. It is not DOWN, it is not lowered, its members
are not released, and a later step may still detach a member from it, attach a member to it or
lower it.

N096  A BOND EVERY ONE OF WHOSE MEMBERS HAS LOST ITS LINK IS NOT DOWN. Its members are still
enrolled by N057, so the no-member test at N097 does not answer. Its counted column is zero, so
it is in reduced standing where its floor is one or more, and where its floor is zero it is
merely short of full membership. Its carry stands vacant either way, by N073.

--- The bond state ladder (N097 through N106) ---

N097  EVERY BOND THE BOND TABLE NAMES takes EXACTLY ONE state out of this closed set of five.
The rungs are weighed IN THIS ORDER and the FIRST one that answers is the state:
      rung 1  it has NEVER been raised at any moment                          UNMADE
      rung 2  it does not stand at the close                                  DOWN
      rung 3  it stands and NO MEMBER IS ENROLLED on its ladder               DOWN
      rung 4  it is in reduced standing at the close                          STINTED
      rung 5  its counted column is BELOW its declared count                  THIN
      rung 6  none of the above                                               FULL
THE NO-MEMBER TEST AT RUNG 3 IS WEIGHED BEFORE THE FLOOR TEST AT RUNG 4, and it asks about
ENROLMENT and not about link standing. That is why N096 holds.

N098  UNMADE is the state of a bond the run never raised and the bond table did not write
raised at the open. Its ladder was never built, so its enrolled and counted columns are both
zero; its declared count and its floor still print.

N099  DOWN is the state of a bond that does not stand at the close, whether it was lowered by a
step or was standing at the open and never raised again, and it is also the state of a bond
that stands with no member enrolled at all. Which of the two happened is carried by the token,
at N103.

N100  STINTED is the state of a bond that stands, holds at least one member, and is in reduced
standing at the close. It stands: it is not DOWN and it is not failed.

N101  THIN is the state of a bond that stands, holds at least one member, is not in reduced
standing, and whose counted column is BELOW the number of members it declares. It is short of
full membership but it is at or above its floor, so it still bears.

N102  FULL is the state of a bond that stands, holds at least one member, is not in reduced
standing, and whose counted column is AT OR ABOVE the number of members it declares.

N103  Every bond prints exactly one RULE TOKEN out of this closed set of seven. Under each
state the rungs are weighed IN THIS ORDER and the FIRST that answers is the token:
      FULL     rung 1  always                                              full.assembled
      THIN     rung 1  always                                              thin.short
      STINTED  rung 1  its counted column is BELOW its floor at the close   stint.floor-lost
               rung 2  its counted column is AT OR ABOVE its floor at the
                       close and the standing was never cleared            stint.held-over
      DOWN     rung 1  it does not stand at the close                      down.lowered
               rung 2  it stands with no member enrolled                   down.no-member
      UNMADE   rung 1  always                                             cold.never-raised

N104  The two STINTED tokens are the visible difference between a bond that is under its floor
NOW and a bond that WAS under its floor and has since regained its members while the standing
was never cleared. stint.floor-lost is the first, stint.held-over is the second, and the
direction is fixed by N103 and never inferred from anything else.

N105  The two DOWN tokens are weighed in the order N103 gives: a bond that does not stand at
the close prints down.lowered even where its ladder is also empty, and down.no-member prints
only for a bond that DOES stand.

N106  A BOND OF EXACTLY ONE DECLARED MEMBER. While that member is enrolled the bond is never
DOWN by the no-member test, whatever its link is doing; where its link is down it is STINTED
under a floor of one or more and THIN under a floor of zero, by N096. Detaching that member
empties the ladder, and from that instant the bond is DOWN with down.no-member while it stands.

--- Bonds at the edges, and the head line's carrying count (N107 through N112) ---

N107  A BOND WHOSE MEMBER FLOOR EQUALS THE NUMBER OF MEMBERS IT DECLARES falls under its floor
the moment one counted member is lost, so it goes STRAIGHT TO STINTED WITHOUT EVER PASSING
THROUGH THIN. Because reduced standing is sticky by N093 it does not come back without a lower
step and a raise step.

N108  A BOND STANDING WITH NO MEMBER ENROLLED IS DOWN, by rung 3 of N097. This holds even where
its member floor is zero, because the no-member test is weighed before the floor test and does
not read the floor at all.

N109  A bond that was never raised prints its declared count from the member table, an enrolled
column of zero, a counted column of zero, its floor from the bond table, and a single hyphen in
the bearer column.

N110  A bond that was lowered prints the enrolled and counted columns its ladder left it with,
by N085, and a single hyphen in the bearer column.

N111  THE CARRYING COUNT on the head line is the number of bonds for which SOME MEMBER BEARS at
the close, that is, the number of bonds whose carry is held. It is NOT the number of bonds that
stand, and it is not the number of bonds that are FULL.

N112  A bond takes no part in any address contest beyond what N094 gives it. Whether it stands,
whether it is STINTED and whether it is DOWN changes nothing about a record declared on it
except through N094; N133 says so from the other side.
