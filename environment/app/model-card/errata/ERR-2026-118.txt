ERR-2026-118 — diagnostics.json
Status: shipping / defines diagnostics artifact

On every successful run, write `/var/lib/.../diagnostics.json` (under --out)
with exactly this schema and field set:

{
  "r_star": <number>,
  "n_users": <int>,
  "n_items": <int>,
  "n_pairs": <int>,
  "fade": 0.994,
  "lambda_schedule": [<number>; T],
  "mean_abs_score": <number>
}

r_star, n_users, n_items, n_pairs refer to the retained catalog after
filtering. lambda_schedule[t-1] is lambda_t for iteration t=1..T under
ERR-2026-097. mean_abs_score is the mean of abs(s) over every catalog
user × catalog item pair after packing (U*I terms; include zeros).
