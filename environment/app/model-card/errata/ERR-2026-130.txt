ERR-2026-130 — Local confidence denominators
Status: shipping / supersedes ERR-2026-071 global R_star inside ALS updates

When forming the confidence for an observation while updating user u:
  R_u = max aggregate count among the retained observed items of u
  (if u has no observations, R_u = 1)
  c = 1 + alpha * log1p(r) / log1p(R_u)

When forming the confidence for an observation while updating item i:
  R_i = max aggregate count among the retained observed users of i
  (if i has no observations, R_i = 1)
  c = 1 + alpha * log1p(r) / log1p(R_i)

diagnostics.json field r_star remains the GLOBAL max over all retained pairs
(as in ERR-2026-118). Do not substitute local maxima into diagnostics.r_star.
