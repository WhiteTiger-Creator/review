ERR-2026-058 — Unit-sphere packing
Status: shipping / supersedes model card serialization + scoring

After the final ALS iteration completes (and only then), L2-normalize every
user factor vector and every item factor vector to Euclidean length 1.0
before writing model.json and before computing query scores, holdout
rankings, diagnostics mean_abs_score, or folds that consume the packed
global model.

If a factor vector has Euclidean norm 0, leave it as the zero vector.

Do not normalize between intermediate ALS iterations. Conditional fade
(if any) applies to un-normalized factors during the loop; packing is last
(before sign canon).
