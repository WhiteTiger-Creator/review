ERR-2026-112 — AP@k denominator
Status: shipping / supersedes base-card AP@k average denominator

AP@k = sum of precision@i over ranks i=1..k where the i-th recommended
item is relevant, divided by min(k, |R|).

If no relevant item appears in top_k, AP@k = 0.

Do not divide by the hit count inside top_k when that hit count differs
from min(k, |R|).
