ERR-2026-071 — Relative log1p confidence (global form — superseded later)
Status: shipping baseline / later errata may localize R_star

Let R_star be the maximum aggregate count r_ui among retained pairs after
catalog filtering. If there are no retained pairs, R_star = 1.

For retained observed pairs with aggregate count r_ui >= 1:
  p_ui = 1
  c_ui = 1 + alpha * log1p(r_ui) / log1p(R_star)

where log1p(x) = ln(1 + x) in natural log.
Unobserved pairs remain implicit with c = 1, p = 0.
