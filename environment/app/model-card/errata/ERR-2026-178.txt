ERR-2026-178 — Confidence ceiling
Status: shipping / extends local confidence from ERR-2026-130

After computing the local relative confidence

  c_raw = 1 + alpha * log1p(r) / log1p(r_local)

clamp it:

  c = min(c_raw, 40.0)

Use c (not c_raw) in every ALS outer-product and rhs accumulation for
both item and user half-steps, including fold remass fits.
