ERR-2026-172 — Item residual blend
Status: shipping / extends item half-step; does not apply to user half-step

Let beta = 0.5.

After the item-factor Cholesky solves for iteration t produce candidate
vectors Y_hat[i] for every item i, REPLACE the working item factors with
the residual blend against the previous item factors Y_prev:

  Y[i] = (1 - beta) * Y_prev[i] + beta * Y_hat[i]

Then apply the conditional fade from ERR-2026-091 to these blended Y
vectors (fade only when lambda_t equals base lambda).

User-factor updates remain full replacement (no residual blend on X).
Fold remass fits use the same item-only blend rule.
