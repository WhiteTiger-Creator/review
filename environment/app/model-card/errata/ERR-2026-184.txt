ERR-2026-184 — Mask observed train items in ranking
Status: shipping / extends holdout ranking and fold MAP ranking only

When building the ranked candidate list for a user u for:
  - holdout precision@k / map@k / ndcg@k
  - fold map_at_k
EXCLUDE every item_id that appears in u's observed pairs in the catalog
being scored (global retained catalog for holdout; remassed fold train
catalog for folds).

Rank the remaining candidates with the usual damped scores and the usual
lower-item_id tie-break. Take the top min(k, |candidates|) items.

If fewer than k candidates remain, evaluate metrics on the shorter list;
precision@k still divides hit count by k (not by list length).

This mask does NOT apply to:
  - scores.json query scoring
  - diagnostics.json mean_abs_score
  - model.json factors

Relevant sets R are unchanged: a holdout-positive or fold-hold item that
is also a train observation for u remains in R, but cannot appear in the
ranked list because of the mask.
