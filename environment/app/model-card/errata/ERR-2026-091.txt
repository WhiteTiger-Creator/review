ERR-2026-091 — Half-step fade (conditional)
Status: shipping / introduces production fade; supersedes unconditional fade notes

fade = 0.994

After the item-factor block of iteration t completes, if and only if
lambda_t equals the base lambda (not 2*lambda), multiply every item factor
vector component-wise by fade.

After the user-factor block of iteration t completes, if and only if
lambda_t equals the base lambda, multiply every user factor vector
component-wise by fade.

Do not fade on iterations where lambda_t = 2*lambda.
Do not apply an extra fade after packing.
