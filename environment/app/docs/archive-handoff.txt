Furnace archive handoff

The QIR files were copied from the controller database after the vendor reader was retired. Frame dimensions in the frames table came from the camera registry, not from the payload decoder. PLC checkpoints are sparse but independent of the archive writer. The reference_samples rows came from blackbody fiducials recorded during each exposure; sigma_c is the certified uncertainty for that reference. The packet grammar is recorded in qir2-packet-format.txt. The metrology contract covers detector repair, eight-pass robust drift correction and covariance propagation, furnace orientation, projective pixel area, robust thermal-load regions, and confidence-qualified thermal regions, Arrhenius uncertainty, and conservative dose. The latest geometry profiles also define temporal correlation for generalized least-squares Arrhenius acceleration and correlated trapezoidal dose.
