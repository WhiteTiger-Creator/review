Matches alternate board activations under a deterministic schedule until checkmate, stalemate, threefold repetition, or the action horizon. Team points combine board results, material still on the boards, and successful relay transfers. Checkmate awards one hundred team points to the mating side. Stalemate is neutral on that board. When a board ends by threefold repetition and the side to move trails on remaining material, that team loses five points.

Ready drop inventory adds two points per ready piece. Overflow transfers that never entered a full queue still credit one material unit each. Each public and graded scenario publishes an acceptance_floor; a seat meets acceptance only when protocol faults and belief faults are zero and team_a points are at least that floor. Long linked campaigns with horizon at least sixteen must also record at least one teammate request action in the authoritative ply log before the terminal message. Horizon scoring uses remaining material and ready inventory when no terminal chess result lands first. Identical engine inputs must reproduce identical normalized score fields. Wall clocks are not used; each seat has a deterministic action budget counted in plies and messages.
