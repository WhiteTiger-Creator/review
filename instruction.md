Fix it so every valid job in `/app/queue` is published exactly once under `/output/signed`, each signature verifies against the key selected by the active configuration, and interrupted work resumes without accepting stale signing state. A clean run, worker replacement, and full service restart must produce equivalent accepted records.

Ambiguous key selection, conflicting reuse of a job identifier, and unsupported signing mechanisms must be rejected without publishing a partial result. Do not export private key material or write the token PIN into `/output` or `/var/log/signing`.
