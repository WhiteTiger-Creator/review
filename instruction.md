The evaluation group needs the contamination audit behind /app/bin/memscope finished, so that every benchmark record named by /app/config/audit.json ends up with a calibrated probability that the model under test memorized it during training. The command form is memscope audit --config followed by a configuration path. Evidence has to come from live answers to the configured endpoint rather than anything particular to the supplied fixture, since the same command gets rerun against records, an adjudicated panel and a service you have not seen. Support modules under /app/src already cover JSON Lines and canonical JSON, probe posting and validation, every probe and study request body, the two overlap indexes, the panel reader and its feature order, a dense linear solver, and the SQLite schema and writer, so the audit logic and the entrypoint are what is left. Everything under /app stays Lua, with no second interpreter and no helper script in another language.

Probe each item with the six families the variants module names, one original, one perturbed and one order probe for every configured seed, one probe for every paraphrase the record carries, and metadata framed and canary probes at the highest configured rarity. Record each request through the store so the probes table holds them in issue order, then read the evidence back out of those rows as means. exact_match_rate is the mean correct over original probes and paraphrase_robustness the same over paraphrase probes, completion_likelihood_gap is the mean original logprob less the mean paraphrase logprob, and perturbation_sensitivity is exact_match_rate less the perturbed mean, floored at zero. The order, metadata and canary families supply order_invariance, metadata_leak and canary_recall as the mean of their own matching column. Sweep every value of the six study axes with one probe per item per value, tag those rows with the axis and the value they were issued at, and report one parameter_study entry of axis, value and mean_signal per value. Each axis reports the signal it moves, so that is the completion logprob for paraphrase distance and benchmark age, canary recall for canary rarity, metadata exposure for prompt framing, and the exact match indicator for answer format and sampling randomness.

Fold that evidence, substring_overlap and semantic_overlap into a contamination probability through a head calibrated on the adjudicated panel the configuration names, whose rows carry the same nine evidence fields and a contaminated flag. Center and scale each panel column by its own mean and its population spread with no sample correction, and put item evidence through that same centering. The model is a logistic fit with an unpenalized intercept whose objective is the mean negative log likelihood over the rows in the fit plus the penalty times the summed squared slopes. Pick the penalty out of the configured grid by leaving one modality out at a time, where a fold contributes the mean negative log likelihood of the rows it held out, a penalty is summarized by the mean across folds, and its standard error is the sample deviation of those fold values over the square root of the fold count. Keep the largest penalty whose summary stays within one standard error of the lowest summary, reading that error at the penalty holding the lowest summary. score is the fitted probability at the kept penalty over the whole panel, while lower and upper move the item log odds by 1.645 leave one out jackknife standard errors either way, that jackknife refitting the panel once per dropped row. Report the fit as calibration holding exactly penalty, mean_log_loss for the summary of the kept penalty, intercept, and coefficients in the order the panel module lists its features.

Cleaning keeps the largest subset of items whose mean upper risk stays within risk_limit, whose size is at least minimum_retention of the benchmark rounded up, and which leaves at least minimum_per_modality records of every modality present. Prefer the lower mean upper risk when sizes tie, and the alphabetically first identifier list after that. Leave /app/output/audit.json holding exactly items, calibration, summary, parameter_study and cleaning, where summary holds exactly item_count and probe_count over all persisted probes, and cleaning holds retained_ids and removed_ids both ascending, retained_fraction and mean_upper_risk. Each item carries id, modality, completion_likelihood_gap, exact_match_rate, perturbation_sensitivity, order_invariance, metadata_leak, canary_recall, paraphrase_robustness, substring_overlap, semantic_overlap, score, lower, upper and decision, which reads keep for a retained record and remove otherwise. Write the retained records to /app/output/cleaned.jsonl and the persisted evidence to /app/output/probes.sqlite. Repeated runs have to reproduce the JSON and JSON Lines bytes exactly, and a malformed configuration or endpoint response has to exit nonzero while leaving completed outputs untouched.
