Reach the exit in every Crystal Cellar Push board using the fewest steps. Each cellar is a small grid with movable crystal blocks, frozen channels, cracked crystal, floor seals, and gates. Walking one square or pushing a block counts as one step. A block pushed onto ice continues in that direction until it reaches the first passable non-ice square. Cracked crystal acts like ice during that push, then collapses and blocks later movement. Gates open exactly while every floor seal has a block on it. If a board has no floor seals, its gates are open.

The board uses `#` for walls, `.` for floor, `@` for the start, `X` for the exit, `o` for crystal blocks, `~` for ice, `c` for cracked crystal, `p` for floor seals, and `D` for gates. The player moves in the four cardinal directions and may push only one adjacent block at a time. A slide is illegal if it would leave the board or meet a wall, closed gate, collapsed tile, or another block before reaching its landing square. Floor, the exit, a floor seal, and an open gate are valid non-ice landing squares. The player cannot walk through any of those obstacles or through a block.

Keep the existing Ruby batch runner in `/app`. The first nonblank line on standard input is the level count. Each level then has a header containing its name, row count, and column count, followed by that many grid rows. Print one line per level in input order, containing exactly the level name and minimum step count, or the name followed by `IMPOSSIBLE` when no legal route reaches the exit. Preserve ordinary walking on boards without blocks or gates. The entry point remains `/app/bin/crystal-cellar-push`.
