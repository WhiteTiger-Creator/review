The host duty-transfer control plane under `/app/environment` should hand the live desk to a new principal in one coherent commit. Drills currently leave the unit drop-in, deskstate custody, ledger, mesh membership slice, Unix-socket fence, and `/app/output` exports disagreeing — incomplete transfers look partly applied, and later commits forget prior state. Fix the sources, rebuild with `/app/environment/scripts/build.sh`, and drive `cabrelay` apply/resume/status against a TOML config and runtime root (default `/app/var`). Hand-written output files alone will not pass.

Use configs under `/app/environment/configs/` (`transfer.toml`, `transfer_alt.toml`, `transfer_invalid.toml`). The `status` subcommand rereads the live drop-in, deskstate, socket, ledger, mesh, and exports each call, prints JSON only to stdout, and never creates or updates `/app/output/status.json`. `apply` and successful `resume` publish `/app/output/status.json`, `/app/output/custody.csv`, and `/app/output/mesh_reconcile.json` from the live surfaces; `apply` also writes `status.json` on rejection with `epoch` still 0 and `finish_reason` neither `applied` nor `resumed`. Successful finishes set `finish_reason` to `applied` or `resumed` respectively. On a successful commit, surfaces name the incoming principal together: `/app/var/units/desk.service.d/override.conf` (or the configured unit) has incoming user, gid, and supplementary groups sorted ascending and comma-joined; `/app/var/deskstate` gets incoming ownership, setgid mode `02750`, and xattr `user.cabrelay.seal` as `epoch:uid:gid`; `/app/var/sockets/desk.sock` is mode `0660` with group equal to the incoming gid (absent while incomplete); `/app/var/ledger/cursor.json` and append-only `/app/var/ledger/journal.ndjson` (each line records attempt `result`) and `/app/var/mesh/slice.json` bump committed `epoch` by one only when drop-in+custody+mesh+socket all commit, keeping the previous seal across a later success. Incomplete mesh stays non-live with empty `digest`; a committed mesh sets `phase` to `live`, publishes holder ids, sorted supp csv, and digest `seal|supp_csv`, remembering the previous digest later. `/app/output/status.json` carries `epoch`, `phase`, `unit_user`, `unit_gid`, `custody_uid`, `custody_gid`, `custody_mode`, `seal`, `socket_mode`, `socket_gid`, `mesh_digest`, `prior_seal`, and `finish_reason`; `/app/output/custody.csv` has one row with `path`, `uid`, `gid`, `mode`, and `seal`; `/app/output/mesh_reconcile.json` carries `epoch`, `seal`, `mesh_digest`, `unit_user`, `custody_uid`, `socket_gid`, and `prior_seal`. Those exports must match the live surfaces, including mesh digest and prior seal.

Rejected principal configs exit non-zero, leave desk ownership alone, create no drop-in or desk socket, and do not publish a live mesh. With `/app/var/ledger/crash.inject` at apply start, the drop-in may exist and `/app/var/ledger/journal.ndjson` may record unfinished work, but the socket stays closed, mesh stays non-live with empty `digest`, and `epoch` does not advance; resume finishes custody, mesh, and socket as one commit with `finish_reason` `resumed`. A second apply against unfinished work exits non-zero with no epoch advance. A later successful apply refreshes every surface for the same incoming principal and preserves the previous seal and digest for history.
