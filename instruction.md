Train a weighted classification tree from the labeled examples in the training TSV and use it to classify the rows in the prediction TSV. Complete the Rust learner at `/app/task_file/src/main.rs`; it is compiled to `/app/task_file/branch` and receives the training TSV, prediction TSV, and config TSV. Training columns are `id`, numeric feature columns, `label`, and positive `weight`; prediction columns are `id` plus the same features. A feature value of `?` is missing. Config has `max_depth<TAB>N` and `min_leaf_weight<TAB>W`.

At each node, consider every midpoint between consecutive distinct nonmissing values for every feature. Choose the split with the largest weighted Gini decrease, breaking equal gains within `1e-12` lexicographically by feature header name and then by lower threshold. Missing training weight is divided between children in proportion to their nonmissing weight at that split. A candidate is invalid when either resulting child has weight below `min_leaf_weight`. Stop at pure nodes, depth `max_depth`, or when no positive-gain split exists. Leaves store weighted label frequencies; equal class weights choose the lexicographically smaller label.

For a missing prediction value, traverse both children using their training-weight proportions and combine their label distributions. Emit exactly `{"predictions":[{"id":"r1","label":"yes","probability":0.75},...]}` in input order; probability is the chosen label's combined probability, finite and rounded to six decimals. Training weights must be positive. Do not modify `/app/task_file/input_data`.
