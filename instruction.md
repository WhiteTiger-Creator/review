The Redeal is a solitaire: counters sit in heaps, and its one move — a redeal — lifts one counter from every heap and drops them as one new heap, so play eventually settles into a recurring endgame. Saved openings sit under `/app/openings/`, one per file naming an identifier and heap sizes; the folder is read-only. The play rules, the canonical form and ordering of positions, and the meaning of every reported quantity are fixed by the binding notes under `/app/docs/`.

A C++17 program at `/app/build/redeal`, built from sources under `/app/src/`, takes exactly two arguments `<openings_dir> <out_dir>` and exits non-zero on a missing directory or a malformed opening. Two reports land in `<out_dir>`. `openings.json` is an object whose `openings` list holds one record per opening: canonical heaps, counters in play, redeals to reach the endgame, endgame length, the endgame as an ordered list of positions, how many positions redeal into it, and whether play can reach it. `sizes.json` is an object whose `sizes` list holds one record per distinct counter total: how many positions it has, how many are never produced by a redeal, the longest settling, and its endgames as a list. Both reports are UTF-8, ASCII-only, two-space indented, keys sorted at every depth, with a trailing newline.
